# BYLINE API contract (v0.2, live)

Backend: FastAPI on `http://127.0.0.1:8000` (`apps/api`, start with `py tools/run.py api`). Vite proxies `/api` and `/healthz`.
All data is synthetic. Coordinates: metres, pitch 105 x 68, `x` 0→105 left→right, `y` 0→68 top→bottom of the canonical pitch.

## SSE events (`/api/sessions/{sid}/stream`)

On connect the server sends `match_state`, `heartbeat`, every `pattern_candidate` so far and their `insight_update`s.

- `heartbeat` (≈1/s and after every control): `{playhead_ms, speed, playing, cursor, duration_ms, server_time}`. The client should extrapolate `playhead_ms += dt*speed` between heartbeats while `playing`.
- `match_event` (id = seq): a MatchEvent: `{event_id, seq, elapsed_ms, period, display_clock, team_id, player_id, type, truth_status, payload, ...}`.
- `match_state` (after headline events and every 2 s): `{seq, elapsed_ms, period:"PRE"|"H1"|"HT"|"H2"|"FT", phase, play_state:"PLAYING"|"STOPPED"|"RESTART_AWARDED"|"VAR_REVIEW"|"ADVANTAGE_ACTIVE"|..., clock:"27:41", minute:"28'", score:{NBA:1,RVC:0}, provisional_score:{...}, goals:[{goal_event_id, team_id, scorer_id, minute, status:"provisional"|"confirmed"|"disallowed"}], cards:[{card_event_id, team_id, player_id, colour:"yellow"|"second_yellow"|"red", minute, reason}], possession_team, attacking_direction:{NBA:"+x",RVC:"-x"}, teams:{NBA:{formation, on_pitch:{slot:player_id}, bench, used_subs, off, sent_off}}, stats:{NBA:{passes, passes_completed, shots, shots_on_target, fouls, corners, offsides}}, var_review:{subject_event_id, check_type, reason}|null, playhead_ms}`.
  - **Score display rule:** show `score`; if `provisional_score` differs, show the provisional value with a clear "VAR CHECK"/"CHECKING" state. Never present a provisional goal as confirmed.
- `pattern_candidate` (id = candidate_id): deterministic detector output `{candidate_id, pattern_type, team_id, detected_at_ms, display_clock, evidence_event_ids, calculated_metrics, factual_summary, confidence_band, unsupported_claims, ...}`.
- `insight_update`: `{candidate_id, pattern_type, team_id, detected_at_ms, display_clock, stage, provider, from_cache, insight|null, trace_summary|null}`.
  - `stage` progression (real, emitted by the agent workflow — do not fake progress): `queued → investigating → analysing → verifying → validating → verified | tentative | rejected`; `tentative` + `insight.verification.checker_name` starting `deterministic_only` means AI unavailable or rejected (show "Detector summary · AI unavailable" style label).
  - `insight` follows `contracts/insight.schema.json`: `title, canonical_claim, why_it_matters, uncertainty, certainty, evidence_event_ids, metric_provenance, verification{verdict,issues,checker_name}, audience_variants{commentator, fan_discover, fan_analyst, studio{template_id, headline, subtitle}}`.
- `replay_reset`: `{playhead_ms, cursor, last_seq}` after seek/reset — clear local event buffers and refetch tracking.

## v0.8 additions (fictional history and live context facts)
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/context?upto_seq=` → `[ContextFact]`, ordered by `as_of_seq`. Only events with `seq <= upto_seq` are used, so pass the session's current seq (no future knowledge; the list is deterministic and ids are stable). 404 for an unknown match.
- `ContextFact = {fact_id, priority:"alert"|"insight"|"quiet", category, subject_kind:"player"|"team"|"match", subject_id, short_text(≤140), detail, rule, trigger_event_id, as_of_seq, provenance:"fictional_seeded", source}`. `trigger_event_id=null` means pre-match context (valid from seq 0, or from the team's `lineup.confirmed` for starters' milestones).
- Categories: `milestone` (appearance, club-goal and derby-goal marks reached today), `goal` (scorer's season/club/derby tally after a CONFIRMED goal), `streak`, `record` (first/longest/biggest since a date), `head_to_head`, `form`, `manager`, `stadium`, `fixtures`, `discipline` (re-uses the player-facts booking/ban rule).
- While the match is live there is at most one `ctx-ifends-{home}-{away}` fact, worded conditionally ("If it ends 2-1, …") and tied to the confirmed score; it is replaced when the score changes and dropped at `match.finished`, when the `ctx-ft-*` result facts appear. A VAR-disallowed goal never produces a scorer fact or moves the projection.

## V3: tactics, What If?, signals (deterministic, no model calls)

- `GET /api/matches/{id}/tactics?upto_seq=`: tactical moments released by `upto_seq`. A `TacticalMoment` has: `moment_id`, `kind` (line_break, overload, option, foul_stops_attack, beaten, high_turnover, between_lines, run, shape_change, press, stretched, response), `label`, `team_id`, `at_ms`, `seq`, `display_clock`, `title`, `observed` [{label, value, unit}], `summary`, `interpretation`, `unknowns[]`, `evidence_event_ids[]`, `focus_player_ids[]`, `frame_ms`, `significance`, `whatif`, `available_ms` / `available_seq` (only released once the data it uses exists), `plain` (fan wording) and `provenance`.
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/tactics/{moment_id}/view`: the moment as a drawable frame: `players` [{pid, team_id, x, y, gk, number, name}], `ball`, `arrows` (evidence actions), `lines` {back_x, mid_x, offside_x}, `replay` {from_ms, to_ms}, `whatif` (or null) and `ai_state` (`verified` | `tentative` | `rejected` | `not_run`; additive key, read from recordings only).
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/tactics/{moment_id}/insight?upto_seq=` (V4): the agents' interpretation of a released moment when a run has been recorded. Returns `{match_id, moment_id, state, insight, provenance, notice}`. `state` is `verified` | `tentative` | `rejected` | `not_run`. `insight` (null unless verified or tentative) has `title`, `canonical_claim`, `why_it_matters`, `uncertainty`, `certainty`, `evidence_event_ids`, `metric_provenance`, `audience` {commentator, studio_title, studio_subtitle, fan_plain, fan_analyst, translations}, `hypothetical_label`, `verifier` (verdict and per-claim checks) and `validator` {passed, issues, attempts}. `provenance` has `provider`, `model_deployment`, `prompt_version`, `input_hash`, `recording_key`, `model_calls`, `input_tokens`, `output_tokens`, `calls[]` (role, latency_ms, tokens, response_id, ledger_slot, started_at), `ledger`, `est_cost_usd`, `started_at`/`finished_at`, `cache_hit`, `served_from: "recording"`. The endpoint never starts a model call in any mode; 404 before the moment is released.
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/tactics/{moment_id}/whatif`: `carrier_id`, `origin`, `actual` {kind, receiver_id, end, outcome, interceptor_id, option}, `alternatives` (at most 2 real options on the same frame: lane clearance, `lane_opponents`, receiver space, progression, opponents bypassed and their ids, offside flag, rating open / contested / blocked / offside), `labels` {history, hypothetical}, `rules`. No probabilities and no continuation of real play.
  - Additive (ADR 10): `continuations` [{`kind` pass | shot | cross, `from_id` (an alternative's receiver, assumed on the ball), `from` [x, y], `label` ("Simulated continuation: … Not what happened; everyone else is assumed still; no outcome is implied."), and one block named after the kind}]. `pass` is an option measured exactly like `alternatives` from the receiver's frozen spot (open or contested, square or forward, never back to the original passer, best 2, plus `rank`). `shot` {`goal_centre`, `posts`, `distance_m`, `angle_deg` (goal mouth seen from the ball), `defenders_in_triangle` (outfield opponents inside ball-to-posts), `goalkeeper_id`, `goalkeeper`, `goalkeeper_in_triangle`}, present only at ≤ 25 m, ≥ 12° and ≤ 1 outfield opponent in the triangle. `cross` {`channel` left | right, `targets` [{pid, x, y, nearest_marker_id, marker_distance_m}], `offside_ids`}, present only from a wide channel in the final third with an onside team-mate in the box. `continuation_rules` states the thresholds. Empty list when the geometry supports nothing. Computed from the decision frame only.
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/signals?upto_seq=`: the prioritised stream. A `Signal` has: `signal_id` (stable), `kind` (goal, card, substitution, var, penalty, milestone, record, context, storyline, tactical), `tier` alert / insight / quiet, `headline`, `text` {commentator, studio, fan}, `audiences[]`, `refs`, `evidence_event_ids[]`, `status` live / corrected / withdrawn, `status_reason`, `version`, `folded` (similar items suppressed into it) and `actions` (show_why, what_if, research).
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/context?upto_seq=`: history-derived context facts (see `docs/data-contracts.md` §5.1).
- SSE `tactical_moment` and `signal` are sent live, on seek and on connect (after `storyline_update` in the burst order). A changed signal is re-sent with a higher `version`; clients keep the newest.
- The studio desk adds `source_kind: "tactical"` and `template_id: "tactical_moment"` packets for insight-tier tactical moments (human approval still required), plus `on_hold` on goal graphics while VAR checks the goal.

## V4: commentator research (dossiers + talking points; deterministic, no model calls)

All five endpoints are pure functions of the fictional history (knowledge base, `HistoryProvider`, `records.py`, context facts) and the synthetic events with `seq <= upto_seq`; nothing later can change a result. `upto_seq` is optional (omitted = whole log; `-1` = before the first event). Every block carries a `scope` string (league only vs league + cup, record window `2016/17 to 2025/26`, "before today" vs "this match"); every payload has `fictional: true` and a `notice`. Answers are memoised per (match, seq, subject) and the full-log versions are warmed at startup (< 150 ms after the first call; the first call at a new seq costs one replay, shared with `/state`). Unknown match, team or player → 404.

- `GET /api/matches/{id}/dossier/players/{player_id}?upto_seq=` →
  `{match_id, as_of:{upto_seq, elapsed_ms, period, minute}, player:{player_id, name, display_name, shirt_number, position, position_word, preferred_foot, age, hometown, academy_graduate, joined_season, fee_note, club:{team_id, name, short_name}}, profile_line, this_match, season, career:{scope, seasons:[{season, club, apps, goals, assists, yellows, reds, clean_sheets|null}]}, totals:{scope, club:{seasons, apps, goals, assists, yellows, reds, clean_sheets|null, club, since}, overall:{...}, clubs:[...]}, derby:{scope, goals_before_today, goals:[{date, season, competition, minute, match}], goals_today}, milestones:[{metric, mark, status:"reached_today"|"within_reach", current, short_by?, scope, text}], injuries:[str], connections:[{kind, player_ids, text}], facts:[Fact], fictional, notice}`.
  - `this_match` (events up to `upto_seq`): `{scope, role:"starting_xi"|"bench"|"not_in_squad"|null (null before the line-up is confirmed), on_pitch_now, minutes_on_pitch, ms_on_pitch, minutes_rule, passes:{completed, attempted, pct}, shots:{total, on_target, off_target, blocked}, goals, goal_minutes, goals_disallowed, goals_awaiting_decision, assists, tackles, interceptions, ball_recoveries, carries, pressures, fouls_committed, fouls_won, offsides, saves, cards:[{colour, minute, reason, status, event_id}], substitution:{on:{minute, replaced, event_id}|null, off:{minute, replaced_by, event_id}|null}, last_on_ball}`. Minutes count the event clock while on the pitch in live periods (from kick-off or coming on, to going off, a dismissal or now).
  - `season`: current season, league, before today (`apps, goals, assists, yellows, reds, clean_sheets, one_booking_from_ban, including_today:{apps, goals}`). Career rows are league appearances only (the fictional record has no per-player cup data).
  - `milestones`: appearance marks (50, 100, 150, ...) within 5, goal marks (25, 50, 100, ...) within 3, derby-goal marks (5, 10, ...) within 2, keeper clean-sheet marks within 2; "reached_today" only once the player has started (confirmed line-up) or come on, or scored a confirmed goal. Example (NBA-09 before the line-up): `"1 short of 100 league appearances for Northbridge."`; after it: `"Today is his 100th league appearance for Northbridge."`.
  - `facts` = the same ranked list as `/players/{id}/facts` (live + seeded).
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/dossier/teams/{team_id}?upto_seq=` →
  `{club:{team_id, name, short_name, code, founded, stadium, formation, kit, today:"home"|"away"}, manager:{name, appointed_season, tenure_start, previous, record, league_record, derby_record}, form, league_form, streaks:{scope, current:[Streak]}, current_season:{played, won, drawn, lost, goals_for, goals_against, points}, seasons:{scope, rows:[{season, position, played, won, drawn, lost, goals_for, goals_against, goal_difference, points, top_scorer, cup}]}, honours:{scope, league_titles:[season], cups:[season], best_league_finish}, biggest:{scope, win_all_competitions, defeat_all_competitions, win_league, defeat_league}, upcoming:{scope, fixtures:[{date, season, competition, stage, venue, stadium, opponent, opponent_id}]}, squad:{lineup_confirmed, formation, players:[{player_id, shirt_number, name, display_name, position, season_apps, season_goals, today, slot, on_pitch, subbed_off, sent_off}]}, this_match:{scope, score, conceded, stats, opponent_stats, possession_rule}}`.
  - Records are `{played, won, drawn, lost, goals_for, goals_against, win_pct, line:"P132 W74 D32 L26", scope}`; the manager record covers all club matches from the tenure start date to before today.
  - Form entries (newest first): `{date, season, competition, stage, venue, stadium, opponent, opponent_id, score, goals_for, goals_against, result, derby}`; `line` is oldest → newest.
  - `Streak = {kind:"unbeaten"|"winless"|"winning"|"losing"|"scoring"|"clean_sheet", scope:"all"|"league", where:"any"|"home"|"away", length, since, rule, longest_in_record, is_longest_in_record, previous_run_as_long_ended, first_since_note}` (only runs of length ≥ 1; `first_since_note` only when notable: no run as long in the record, or none within the previous year).
  - Honours are computed from the record window only (league title = position 1, cup = "Winners"). The current season's league position is not in the data and is not shown.
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/dossier/h2h?upto_seq=` → `{rivalry, all_time:{scope, meetings, wins:{NBA, RVC}, draws} (published fictional totals, not itemised), record_window:{scope, ...Record} (22 itemised league + cup derbies, from the home club's side), teams:{NBA|RVC:{record, at_home, away_from_home, biggest_win, last_win, current_run:[{kind, length, since}]}}, last_meetings:{scope, meetings:[{date, season, competition, home_id, away_id, home, away, venue, score, home_goals, away_goals, scorers:[{team_id, name, minute, player_id|null, current_squad}], sent_off, referee, attendance}]} (6, newest first), leading_scorers:{scope, players:[{player_id, name, team_id, derby_goals_before_today, last_derby_goal, goals_today}]}, today:{scope, score}}`.
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/dossier/match` → `{competition, season, matchday, kickoff_local, timezone, venue, home, away, stadium:{name, capacity, record_attendance, record_match, home_club, home_league_record}, officials:[{official_id, name, role, record?}] (the referee carries {games_this_season, yellows_this_season, reds_this_season, penalties_this_season, career_games, cautions_per_game, derbies_in_record, last_derby, scope}), context:[ContextFact] (pre-match only: trigger_event_id null), ruleset:{...Ruleset, notes:[str], league_disciplinary_rule}, scenario, data_notice}`.
- `GET /api/matches/{id}/talking-points?upto_seq=&limit=8` (limit 1–30) → `{match_id, upto_seq, talking_points:[TalkingPoint], fictional, notice}`.
  - `TalkingPoint = {id (stable, e.g. "tp-ctx-goal-NBA-09-E0761", "tp-storyline-…", "tp-yellow-<card event id>"), text (one sentence, ≤ 140 chars), detail|null, kind:"player"|"team"|"h2h"|"record"|"streak"|"milestone"|"discipline"|"manager"|"stadium"|"tactical"|"storyline", subject_ids, why, source:"fictional history"|"this match"|"tracking", at_ms, priority (number, higher first), fictional:true}`.
  - Candidates: context facts valid at `upto_seq` (event-triggered ones get a boost that fades over 10 match minutes), the best strong research fact (relevance ≥ 0.7) for up to three players most involved in the last 3 match minutes, players on a caution who are still on the pitch, DEVELOPING/STRONG/CONFIRMED storylines, up to two significant tactical moments from the last 5 minutes, and the referee's season record. A context fact about a recently involved player is boosted and its `why` names the involvement (e.g. `"N. Vantreight had a shot at 28' (goal) in the last 3 minutes"`, `"G. Ndiaye-Shaw was involved in 4 of Riverton's last 5 possessions"`); other `why` values: `"New at 28': follows the goal"`, `"From the confirmed line-up"`, `"On the pitch now"`, `"Pre-match background"`.
  - Sorted by `priority` desc then `id`; near-identical texts (token overlap ≥ 0.7) keep only the higher-ranked one. Same `upto_seq` → same list.

### V4 match-state stats (new keys only)

`match_state.stats[team]` keeps `passes, passes_completed, shots, shots_on_target, fouls, corners, offsides` and adds: `shots_off_target` (off target or woodwork), `shots_blocked`, `saves` (`save` events by the team's keeper), `throw_ins`, `goal_kicks`, `free_kicks` (direct + indirect; all three from `restart.awarded` to the team), `tackles`, `interceptions`, `possession_ms`, `possession_pct` (share of in-play time between events while the team had the ball; `null` before any; the two teams sum to 100), `yellow_cards` (active first cautions), `red_cards` (second-yellow or straight-red dismissals), `substitutions`. The recap's `stats` keep the original seven keys.

Not in the synthetic event log, so not offered: xG, distance covered, aerial duels, clearances, dribbles. `own_goal`, `challenge`, `turnover`, `advantage.recalled` and `disciplinary.reviewed` are in the event schema but never occur in the shipped logs.
